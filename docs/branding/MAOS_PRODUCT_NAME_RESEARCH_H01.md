# MAOS H01 — Independent product-name research (no purchases, no registrations)

**Task:** H01 "Research independent commercial product names" (Phase 7). **Date:** 2026-11-03. **Scope:** research only. Nothing was bought, registered or reserved; no domain, trademark or store action was taken. H02 (availability) and H03 (branding proposals) follow; H04 (apply the brand) is the owner's decision.

## Why a new name

"MAOS" is an internal working name (Marketing & Operations System) and "Soldado" is the agency. A product sold to other agencies needs a name that is independent of the agency, works in German, English and Arabic, is pronounceable on the phone, and does not collide with an existing software product.

## Criteria (scored 0–2 each)

1. **Distinct** — no existing software product, GmbH or app with the same name in a web search (trademark registers are H02).
2. **Pronounceable** in German, English and Arabic without explanation; no umlauts, ß or hyphens.
3. **Meaning** that points at agency operations (work, rhythm, course, workshop) without describing one feature.
4. **Short** — two to three syllables, under ten letters.
5. **Brandable** — works as a wordmark and a one-letter mark; unlikely to be a dictionary word that competitors also use.
